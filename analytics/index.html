how I work

  1. step 1

    Pain points first, then solutions.

    Before building anything, I find out what decision someone is struggling to make and what's in the way. The dashboard comes last.

  2. step 2

    Define the metric before the visual.

    If "active customer" means three things across three reports, no chart fixes that. Clear definitions come first.

  3. step 3

    Build for the person using it.

    A report nobody understands isn't finished. I document, train, and revise based on how people actually use what I build.
