about

My career has run on three tracks in roughly equal measure: analytics, instructional design, and teaching. They turned out to be one job seen from different angles.

As an analyst, I learned that a correct number isn't finished until someone can act on it. That's why so much of my analytics work became stakeholder enablement: presentations, documentation, and training that helped people use what I built. As an instructional designer, I learned to sequence ideas and design visuals so the reader's attention goes where it should. And as a teacher, I learned to spot where understanding breaks down before it does.

Together, that means I build data products people trust and use.
