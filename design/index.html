how I design

  1. step 1

    Start with the learner's next reliable move.

    In technical subjects, early momentum matters. I find the first thing a learner can do with confidence and build from there.

  2. step 2

    Sequence for understanding.

    I map dependencies and common misconceptions so practice builds fluency instead of reinforcing gaps.

  3. step 3

    Assess to create feedback, not just scores.

    Checks for understanding should show what to do next, for both the learner and the instructor.
